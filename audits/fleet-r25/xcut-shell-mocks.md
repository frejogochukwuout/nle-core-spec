## Verdict

The user's ask is confirmed at corpus scale: **the two shell mocks are the single richest under-tapped reference the corpus has, and the corpus's citation machinery is one round behind them.** The healthy half is real — the save chip, undo law, transport, context menus, cheat sheet, the mini's whole §16 contract, the color W-B references, and the K3/VLM adoption are all spec'd and mock-aligned (verdict rows 9-19). But the sweep found **eight MOCK-ONLY surface families beyond the edit modes** — the source-preview chrome + source in/out marks (the 4-point family's input surface, spec'd nowhere), the deliver RangeBand + its three-writer loop-seam law, marker v2 + clip markers (C33), captions (C34), the FX page + its density law, the 5-page dock divergence, and the context-menu micro-grammar — every one of them shipped, tested (15+8+24+9+9+17+8 pins respectively), and registered only in the mock's own ledgers. The pattern is systemic, not accidental: the mock has a working proposal channel (`.agents/SPEC-REVISION-CANDIDATES.md` C33→C58) whose rows have sat unadopted for up to five rounds because nothing tracks them — while each fleet audit re-derives the same evidence at its own cost. The fix is cheap and this round's: one standing **reference-register** (surface | mock home | test pins | owning spec | citation form | divergence rule) at the spec root, seeded by this audit's 19 families, tied to the R23 K3 row so the VLM net, the LAW-NET re-expression, and the D26.4 retirement triggers all key on it — plus four doc-level rulings (XMOCK-4/5/6/7) that convert the mock's oldest pending proposals into law or registered rejections. Nothing in the mocks contradicts the engine-wins law (D22b holds everywhere it applies); the divergence register just needs to be written down. The mocks keep their role as design references until the app matches them — the register is what makes that retirement checkable.
